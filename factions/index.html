Zglenglo

THE BROOD

The Zglenglo don't build, they proliferate. Their strength is mass: cheap units produced in a constant stream, a hive that evolves in tiers, and a creep spread that extends their vision. Playing Zglenglo means trading individual quality for sheer overwhelming numbers — all the way up to the level-3 Brood Titans.

Helias

THE HELIAS

The Helias fight behind shields that regenerate out of combat, rewarding hit-and-run engagements and micro-management. Expensive but powerful units, warp mobility, and ramping energy weapons: a demanding faction that punishes mistakes, but has a very high ceiling.

HUMANS

The human faction is built on industry and balance. A robust economy feeds a mixed army — infantry, armor, aviation and support — with no glaring weakness. It's the most readable faction to learn the game with, and the most flexible against any opponent.
