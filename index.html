// StarLite II

A real-time strategy game in your browser

Harvest, build, evolve your army and crush the enemy — solo campaign, skirmish against the AI, or online 1v1 duels. Three factions, 39 units, deterministic lockstep multiplayer. No install, nothing to download.

01 / ECONOMY

Harvest to dominate

Every faction starts with its harvesting line. Secure resources before projecting your army.

02 / ARMY

Three doctrines

The Zglenglo's numbers, the Helias shields, human versatility. Three incompatible ways to win.

03 / DUEL

Online multiplayer

Face another player in real time over a deterministic lockstep netcode, right in the browser.

Choose your species

THE BROOD

Zglenglo

Evolution · regeneration · numbers

The Zglenglo don't build, they proliferate. Their strength is mass: cheap units in a constant stream, a hive that evolves in tiers, and creep that extends their vision…

THE HELIAS

Helias

Shields · warp · focused energy

The Helias fight behind shields that regenerate out of combat, rewarding hit-and-run play and micro. Expensive but powerful units with a very high skill ceiling…

HUMANS

Humans

Armor · industry · combined arms

The human faction is built on industry and balance. A robust economy feeds a mixed army with no glaring weakness — the most flexible faction against any opponent…
